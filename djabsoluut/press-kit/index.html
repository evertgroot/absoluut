Biography

DJ Absoluut takes pop music seriously and has serious fun playing it. The Dutch DJ has been mixing iconic hits, nostalgic anthems, overlooked gems and club remixes since 2008. His sets move between pop, disco and house, with a particular love for strong female vocals. He loves hearing a dance floor sing along to a familiar hit, but gets just as excited about introducing people to something new.

Together with fellow DJ TMORGZ, he co-founded BOPS, Amsterdam’s queer pop party at The Other Side. They’ve since organized more than 20 editions. DJ Absoluut’s appearances include Paradiso, A’DAM Tower, Amsterdam Pride, Club NYX, Tropikali and Milkshake Festival. He has also played Antwerp Pride, with Milan appearances at Q Club, Euforia and LeccoMilano.

Technical Rider

Please provide

Players & Mixer

  • 2× (minimum) Pioneer CDJ-2000NXS2 or higher.
  • 1× Pioneer DJM-900NXS2 or higher.
  • All players must have a stable Pro DJ Link ethernet connection.
  • Please ensure all firmware is updated to the latest version for compatibility.

Audio

  • 2× high-quality booth monitors, on the left and right side of the booth, with independent volume control on the mixer and no distortion.

Light

  • Booth lighting must not limit the visibility of the CDJ screens or the mixer.
  • A small, adjustable, neutral-colored lamp to illuminate the players and mixer.

Additional

  • Electricity: at least 1 free outlet in the booth, within arm’s length of the players and mixer.
  • USB access: easy access to insert USB sticks into the players.
  • Troubleshooting: a qualified technician must be on-site during setup and the set.

DJ provides

  • USB sticks, prepared with the latest version of Rekordbox.
  • Sennheiser HD 25 II headphones with 3.5 mm jack.

Questions about the setup? Send an email.
