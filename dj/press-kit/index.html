Biography

DJ Absoluut has been bringing his love for pop music to dance floors since 2008. His sets move between pop, disco and house, combining iconic hits, nostalgic anthems, overlooked gems and club remixes, with a particular love for strong female vocals.

Together with fellow DJ TMORGZ, he co-founded BOPS, Amsterdam’s queer pop party celebrating music, pop culture and community. Absoluut has played events including Milkshake Festival, Tropikali Festival, Woodpop in Antwerp and Club NYX.

Technical Rider

Please provide

Players & Mixer

  • 2× (minimum) Pioneer CDJ-2000NXS2 or higher.
  • 1× Pioneer DJM-900NXS2 or higher.
  • All players must have a stable Pro DJ Link ethernet connection.
  • Please ensure all firmware is updated to the latest version for compatibility.

Audio

  • 2× high-quality booth monitors, on the left and right side of the booth, with independent volume control on the mixer and no distortion.

Light

  • Booth lighting must not limit the visibility of the CDJ screens or the mixer.
  • A small, adjustable, neutral-colored lamp to illuminate the players and mixer.

Additional

  • Electricity: at least 1 free outlet in the booth, within arm’s length of the players and mixer.
  • USB access: easy access to insert USB sticks into the players.
  • Troubleshooting: a qualified technician must be on-site during setup and the set.

DJ provides

  • USB sticks, prepared with the latest version of Rekordbox.
  • Sennheiser HD 25 II headphones with 3.5 mm jack.

Questions about the setup? Get in touch.
